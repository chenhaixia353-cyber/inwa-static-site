  The Box Hema store at Kunming Shuncheng Shopping Center is positioned as a store themed around local culture, and INWA was responsible for the scene design of its key areas.

  The store sits on the basement level of the Wangfujing Department Store at Shuncheng Shopping Center in Kunming. Its main entrance opens onto the sunken plaza of the ground-floor main street, and a secondary entrance connects directly to the metro station, covering roughly 4,000 square metres.

  Shuncheng Shopping Center was built on the site of the old Shuncheng street, a stop on the Tea Horse Road and a trading hub in its prime; the shops and tea houses that once prospered there remain an inseparable part of its identity. The scene design therefore needed to carry the weight of eight hundred years of local history. We used the Tea Horse Road as the thread running through the soft furnishing, in the hope of making the store a Kunming-exclusive destination and a new landmark for Shuncheng culture.

盒马鲜生昆明顺城购物中心店，设计定位为地方特色文化主题店，我们本次主要负责重点区域的场景设计。 店面位于昆明顺城购物中心的王府井百货的负一层，主入口位于一楼主街下沉广场，次入口与地铁站相通。使用面积约4000㎡。

项目定位：顺城购物中心建于顺城老街原址，是茶马古道途经重镇，166商行、百年布庄茶庄的繁荣景象是顺城不可分隔的部分。本项目的场景设计需承载和体现顺城800年历史的厚度感，我们以“茶马古道”为主线进行软装贯穿，力图将本项目打造为昆明独有的、顺城文化的网红打卡地。
