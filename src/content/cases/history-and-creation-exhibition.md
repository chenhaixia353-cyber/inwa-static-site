  The “History and Imagination” exhibition at OCT Suhe Bay was curated with Shi Jian Cultural Heritage. It took the region of Suhe Bay from a full archaeological survey to content planning, and from regional documents and surviving artefacts to a multi-dimensional reconstruction of time and space, presenting both the history and the future of the area.

  During the design of the exhibition space, the white surface layer inside the Shanghai Industrial Bank warehouse was stripped away to reveal the reinforced-concrete structure and the forest of octagonal columns behind it, presenting history in an original and unadorned setting. The buildings and history of the Shanghai General Chamber of Commerce and the Tin Hau Temple were reconstructed in the sense of “spatial archaeology.” The exhibition proposes the century of upheaval in Suhe Bay as a driving force for the regeneration of the district, and reflects on a city structure transformed overnight by the rise of Pudong.

华侨城苏河湾“历史与创想主题展”是联合一石文化史建策划的展览，从苏河湾区域全方位考古到内容策划，从区域文献和遗留实物到多维度时间空间再造体验，展现苏河湾区域的历史与未来。展览空间的设计过程中，将上海实业银行仓库室内的白色表层去除，凸显出建筑构造中的钢筋混凝土表面和八角柱林，在原始和朴素的环境中呈现历史。上海总商会和天后宫的建筑和历史得到“空间考古学”意义上的重建。展览提出以苏河湾激荡的百年史作为区域再生动力，积极应对因浦东崛起而突变的城市格局的思考。
