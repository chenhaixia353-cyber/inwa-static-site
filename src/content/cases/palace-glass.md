  Treasures of Glass from the Palace Museum Collection is a cultural heritage exhibition series organized by Guardian Art Center in collaboration with the Palace Museum. It marks the first large-scale external display of the Palace Museum's collection of Chinese and Western glass artifacts from the 17th to 20th centuries, along with related research achievements.

  By the Qing Dynasty, Chinese glassmaking had reached its zenith in terms of vibrant colors, diverse forms, and intricate craftsmanship, representing the pinnacle of ancient China's glass production history.

  The design draws inspiration from the shapes and techniques of glass art, transforming iconic exhibit pieces into central visual elements. Various glass vessels are set against a red backdrop, with delicate gradients of red, orange, and yellow flowing along their contours, creating a refined texture that shifts with light. The composition of red and blue blocks abstractly recreates the high-temperature melting and cooling-solidification process of glassmaking.

故宫博物院藏玻璃精品展是嘉德艺术中心与故宫博物院合作的传统文化系列展，也是故宫博物院藏17至20世纪中西玻璃文物及研究成果首次大规模在院外展出。

至清代，中国玻璃制造在色彩、造型与工艺上都达到了古代玻璃生产史的高峰。

设计从玻璃艺术的造型与工艺出发，将代表性展品转化为核心视觉元素。玻璃器皿被置于红色形体中，红、橙、黄随造型流动，呈现随光线变化的细腻质感；红蓝色块的组合则抽象再现玻璃烧制时高温熔融与冷却凝固的过程。

展览标题字体以玻璃吹制工艺为灵感，笔画端头像裹上玻璃液体，整体呈现圆润、温和的气质。
